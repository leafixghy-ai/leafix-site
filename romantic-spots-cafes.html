Guwahati evenings
for two people.

Where to go, what to do, and where to sit with a good cup of tea when you don't want to rush anywhere.

Spots worth visiting

The Brahmaputra alone is reason enough for an evening in Guwahati.

Riverside — free

Brahmaputra Ghat, Uzan Bazar

The best free thing in Guwahati. Sit on the steps as the river turns golden — the Brahmaputra is enormous here, with boats and evening light and the North Guwahati hills across. Good for about an hour around sunset. The ferry to Umananda Island leaves from near here if you want to keep moving.

~12 min

Park — free

Dighalipukhuri Park

A large tank in the middle of the city with a walkway around it. Peaceful in the morning, more active in the evenings. Boating available on the tank. More of a slow, quiet walk than a destination — good for winding down at the end of a day.

~15 min

Island temple — ferry

Umananda Island

A small Shiva temple on an island in the Brahmaputra, reached by a short ferry from Uzan Bazar ghat. The island is small but the view of the river from it is good. Best in the morning or early afternoon — the ferry stops running in the evening.

~15 min + ferry

Hilltop temple

Kamakhya Temple, Nilachal Hill

Not typical date territory, but the view of the Brahmaputra from the Nilachal Hill — especially at dawn — is one of the best you'll get in this city. Come for the temple, stay for the panorama. Go early on a weekday for the shortest queues.

~20 min

Cafe

Cafes near GS Road & Bhangagarh

Guwahati's cafe scene has grown fast. The GS Road corridor and Bhangagarh area have the highest concentration — good coffee, quieter interiors, more atmosphere than the older restaurants in the city. Check current Google Maps reviews before going as openings and closings are frequent.

~15 min

Late evening

Pan Bazaar street food

Guwahati's busiest street food stretch. Paan, jhal muri, rolls, momos. Doesn't really get going until after 7pm. Crowded but alive — the kind of place that's more fun in a pair than alone.

~15 min
