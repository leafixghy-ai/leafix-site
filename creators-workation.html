For people who work from wherever

A full apartment. Quiet building. Fast internet.

Most hotels in Guwahati aren't set up for a 10-hour work session. The lobby is noisy. The Wi-Fi is shared. There's nowhere to make a proper meal. Leafix is a private apartment — your own kitchen, your own space, a connection that actually holds for calls.

Kalapahar is a residential neighbourhood — it runs on the schedule of people who live there, not tourists. That means quiet during the day, less traffic than central Guwahati, and a grocery shop within 5 minutes on foot.

The apartment as a backdrop

The interior at Leafix is styled around biophilic design — trailing plants, large-leaf plants on shelves, rattan and wood furniture, warm lighting. There's no extra charge for using the space for personal content creation during your stay.

Reels, YouTube B-roll, photography, podcast recording — guests have used the apartment for all of it. The living area has the most visual depth. The window light in the morning is good.

If you're shooting for a brand campaign, get in touch directly before booking.

Common questions

A few things people ask.

Is the Wi-Fi good enough for video calls and uploads?

Yes. The connection is on a broadband plan suited for video calls, large uploads, and concurrent streaming. Speed details are shared on confirmation.

Can I use the apartment as a shoot location?

The apartment is styled with plants, warm lighting, rattan and wood — many creators use it as a shoot backdrop. No extra charge for personal content creation during your stay.

Is there a dedicated workspace?

There's a table set up for laptop work in the living area. The apartment is quiet enough that most guests work from wherever they're comfortable — the couch, the table, the floor.

Can I stay for multiple weeks?

Extended stays are welcome. Guests staying 7 nights or more usually receive a reduced nightly rate — check via the booking link for current pricing.

Is there anyone else in the apartment?

No. It's a private 1BHK — entirely yours for the duration. No other guests, no shared spaces, no host on the premises.
