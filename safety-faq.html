Everything you need to know before you arrive — verification, privacy, rules, and what to do in an emergency.

Before you arrive

Unmarried couples are welcome. Here's the full process.

Leafix does not ask for a marriage certificate. The only ID requirement is Aadhaar — one per guest. That's it. The check-in is handled by a door code, there's no reception desk, and no one is present in the apartment during your stay.

1

Book your dates

Select dates using the picker on any page. You'll receive a WhatsApp message to confirm.

2

Send Aadhaar photos

Both guests share a photo of their Aadhaar card via WhatsApp. This is the only ID required — no other documents needed.

3

Receive your door code

A few hours before check-in, you'll get the apartment door code and building entry instructions on WhatsApp.

4

Arrive at any time

No host meeting required. Enter with your door code, the apartment is ready. Check-out works the same — lock up and leave.

House rules

A few things to keep in mind.

Noise

It's a residential building. Keep noise down after 10 PM. Normal conversation and TV are fine at any hour.

Smoking

No smoking inside the apartment. The building exterior can be used.

Guests

Only the booked guests may stay overnight. Brief visits from others during the day are fine.

Pets

Not allowed by default. Contact us before booking if you're travelling with a pet.

Plants

They're part of what makes the apartment — please don't rearrange or remove them.

Check-out

Leave the kitchen roughly as found. Towels and linen can be left on the bathroom floor.

Emergency contacts

If something goes wrong.

Host WhatsApp number is shared at confirmation. For medical emergencies, Gauhati Medical College is approximately 15 minutes from the apartment. GNRC Hospitals and Nemcare are also nearby.

Police: 100 · Ambulance: 108 · Fire: 101

Common questions

FAQ.

Are unmarried couples allowed at Leafix GHY?

Yes. Leafix welcomes unmarried couples. Both guests submit Aadhaar before check-in and that's the full requirement. No marriage certificate is asked for.

What ID is required to check in?

Aadhaar card for both guests. Shared via WhatsApp before check-in. No hotel-style physical submission — just a photo of the ID.

How does check-in work?

Self check-in via door code. The code is sent to you before arrival. No host needs to be present. You can arrive at any time.

Is the apartment private?

Completely. It's a private 1BHK — no shared corridors with other guests, no host on the premises. You and your guest are the only people in the apartment.

Are there quiet hours?

It's a residential building, so loud music or noise after 10 PM is not appropriate. Normal conversation and TV volume are fine at any hour.

What's the cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms are shared at the time of booking confirmation. Generally: cancellations 48 hours before check-in receive a full refund. Later cancellations may be partial.

Is smoking allowed?

Not inside the apartment. The building exterior can be used.

Are pets allowed?

Not currently. Please get in touch before booking if you're travelling with a pet — exceptions may be possible depending on the situation.
