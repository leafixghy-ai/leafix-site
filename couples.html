A private stay for couples in Guwahati.
No reception desk. No questions.

Leafix GHY is a self check-in 1BHK in Kalapahar — the whole apartment is yours, the door code is yours, and nobody else has a key. A couple-friendly space in a quiet residential neighbourhood, 15 minutes from the railway station.

The problem

Most hotels in Guwahati make you feel like you're being watched the moment you walk in.

A reception that checks IDs loudly. Staff who knock without warning. Shared corridors where everyone notices everyone else. If you're searching for a couple friendly stay in Guwahati, you've probably already run into this. The city has options — but most of them weren't built with privacy in mind.

Leafix is different. You enter using a door code sent to your phone before arrival. The apartment is yours from check-in to check-out. Nobody knocks. Nobody lingers. The building is residential and quiet — neighbours are neighbours, not hotel staff.

Where you are

Kalapahar — the part of Guwahati that still feels like a neighbourhood.

Leafix sits in Kalapahar, a quiet, leafy area on the southern edge of central Guwahati. Not a tourist strip. Not a commercial hub. Just tree-lined streets, a few good local restaurants, and the kind of quiet that means you can actually hear each other talk.

Uzan Bazar — Guwahati's best stretch for evening walks, riverside views, and cafes — is about 20 minutes away. The railway station is 15 minutes. Most of what you'd actually want to visit in the city is in between.

Before you book

We verify every guest. That's how the privacy works.

Leafix is a private apartment in a residential building. To keep it safe for everyone — including you — we confirm identity for all guests before sending the door code. Send an Aadhaar photo on WhatsApp for each guest after booking. We confirm within a few hours and that's the last you'll hear from us until checkout.

No relationship questions. No check-in desk interrogation. Verification is security, not surveillance.

Common questions

Things couples usually ask before booking.

Are unmarried couples allowed?

Yes. We check identity for security — Aadhaar or PAN for every guest — but we ask no questions about your relationship. The apartment is yours and the stay is private.

Is the entire apartment just for us?

Yes — the full 1BHK is yours for the duration of your stay. No shared rooms, no other guests in the apartment, no host living on-site.

How does self check-in work?

Once booking is confirmed and Aadhaar verification is done over WhatsApp, we send the door code a few hours before your arrival time. You let yourself in — no reception, no host at the door.

Can we check in late at night?

Self check-in means no one is waiting up for you. Arrive any time after your confirmed check-in hour. Just message us on WhatsApp if plans change so we know to expect you.

Is there a kitchen?

Yes — a basic kitchen with appliances is available. Good for morning tea, light cooking, or whatever you had in mind.

What are the check-out rules?

Check-out is 11am. Lock up and leave — no formal checkout process. Message us on WhatsApp if you need a late checkout and we'll check availability.
