Leafix GHY / Medical Stay

A quiet place to rest in Guwahati.
Near the hospitals. Away from the noise.

Leafix GHY is a private 1BHK in Kalapahar — a residential area roughly 15 minutes from Gauhati Medical College. Self check-in, kitchen for cooking patient meals, and a neighbourhood that's genuinely quiet.

Who stays here

A stay built for people who didn't plan on being in Guwahati.

Medical visits rarely go exactly to plan. Flight times change. Hospital appointments shift. Discharge gets delayed. The last thing you need is a stay that adds to the friction — a reception desk with fixed hours, no kitchen to cook in, neighbours who party late.

Leafix GHY works for attendants rotating in and out of hospital duty, families of patients who need a quiet place to sleep, patients recovering before or after a procedure, and doctors or medical professionals visiting Guwahati for a short engagement.

Good to know

We verify every guest. ID on WhatsApp before the door code is sent.

Leafix is in a residential building. Before we send the door code, we confirm Aadhaar for each guest over WhatsApp. It takes a few hours. That's the only step before you're inside — no reception desk, no form to fill at arrival.

For longer stays or last-minute extensions, message us directly on WhatsApp. Medical situations change — we're flexible where we can be.

Common questions

What people usually ask before a medical visit booking.

How far is it from Gauhati Medical College?

Approximately 15 minutes by auto or cab. GNRC, Hayat and Nemcare are 15–20 minutes away.

Is there a kitchen to cook patient food?

Yes — gas stove, utensils and a fridge. You can cook patient-specific meals, store medicines that need refrigeration, or just make chai at 3am.

Can we check in at unusual hours?

Yes. Self check-in means you receive the door code before arrival — there's no reception window and no one to wait for. Arrive whenever your journey brings you here.

Is it quiet enough for someone recovering?

Kalapahar is a residential area — not a commercial street or a market zone. It's genuinely quiet during the day and at night.

Can we extend the stay if the hospital visit takes longer?

Message us on WhatsApp and we'll check availability. We accommodate last-minute extensions where we can — medical timelines are unpredictable.

Is there a pharmacy nearby?

Yes, roughly 5 minutes on foot.
